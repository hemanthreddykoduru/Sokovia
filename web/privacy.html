Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

At Sokovia (sokovia.hemanthreddy.tech), accessible from https://sokovia.hemanthreddy.tech, your privacy and data security are our core priorities. This Privacy Policy documents the types of information collected and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information necessary to provide you with a high-performance native macOS virtual drive:

  • Account Information: Your email address and optional display name when you register or sign in.
  • Payment Information: When you subscribe to a paid tier, billing details are processed securely by our certified payment gateway (Razorpay). We never store your full credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.
  • Device Information: Basic macOS operating system version to verify native WebDAV and VFS mount compatibility.

2. Zero-Knowledge File Privacy & Encryption

Your files are end-to-end encrypted directly on your Mac using standard AES-GCM-256 before they leave your computer. The encryption keys remain strictly on your Mac's secure Keychain. Neither Sokovia nor our cloud storage provider (Cloudflare R2) has access to your private encryption keys or the contents of your files.

3. Cloudflare R2 Cloud Storage

Your encrypted data is stored in isolated, private buckets on Cloudflare R2 infrastructure. Cloudflare R2 provides enterprise-grade reliability and zero-egress data transfer security.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provision and maintain your isolated cloud storage bucket.
  • To process billing and payment receipts through Razorpay.
  • To notify you of account security, renewals, or important service updates.
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot connection issues.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times. If you delete files from your Sokovia drive, they are preserved in your retention bin for 30 days before permanent deletion. If you close your account, your dedicated cloud bucket and all associated metadata are permanently erased.
