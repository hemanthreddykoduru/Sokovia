[ 03 · Verification & Security ] Three Layers of Assurance

Every byte is verified and authenticated.

From the moment a file leaves your application to when it sits in your private bucket, cryptographic integrity is preserved.

LAYER 01

Client-Side HKDF Key Derivation

Your passphrase never leaves your Mac. A 256-bit symmetric key is derived locally via HKDF-SHA256 with isolated salting.

LAYER 02

Authenticated Ciphertext (GCM)

Each chunk is sealed with a 12-byte Nonce and 16-byte authentication tag. Any tampering in transit immediately fails decryption.

LAYER 03

Private Bucket Isolation

Every account receives an isolated Cloudflare R2 bucket with dedicated credentials. No shared databases, no mixed multi-tenant silos.
