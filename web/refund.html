Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

We want you to be completely satisfied with Sokovia. This Cancellation and Refund Policy outlines how cancellations and refund requests are handled for subscriptions processed through Razorpay.

1. 3-Day Money-Back Guarantee (First Payment)

If you purchase a paid storage plan on Sokovia and find that the macOS virtual drive does not work as expected for your workflow, you can request a full 100% refund within 3 days (72 hours) of your initial payment.

  • To request a refund, simply send an email to reddykoduruhemanth@gmail.com with your registered email and Razorpay Payment ID.
  • Refunds are approved and initiated within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Once initiated, the refund will be credited back to your original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Credit/Debit Card) within 5 to 7 business days as per Razorpay standard banking timelines.

2. Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel your recurring subscription at any time without penalty:

  • You can cancel directly from your Sokovia web dashboard under the Account or Plan section, or by contacting customer support.
  • Upon cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of the current paid billing cycle. You will not be billed again.
  • Subsequent renewal charges are non-refundable once the billing period has started.

3. What Happens to Your Data on Cancellation

We never delete your files upon cancellation. If your subscription expires, your account simply transitions to read-only access. You can continue to view, open, and download all your existing files. Uploading new files is paused until you select a plan.
